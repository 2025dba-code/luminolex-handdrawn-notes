# Social Publishing Rules

## Douyin

Generate:
- title
- short description
- hashtags
- cover headline

Title:
- direct
- cognitive tension
- clear benefit
- not exaggerated clickbait

Description:
- 1–3 short paragraphs
- explain the management / coaching problem
- surface one strong insight
- avoid overexplaining the whole episode

## Xiaohongshu

Generate:
- title
- post body
- keywords / hashtags
- cover headline

Title:
- conversational
- relatable
- curiosity-driven
- still professional

Body:
- start from a recognizable workplace situation
- explain the insight
- include 2–4 memorable takeaways
- keep a reflective tone

## Cover headline

Cover headline should not simply repeat the title.

Good pairing:

Cover:
“为什么没人早点告诉我？”

Title:
“真正有效的反馈，为什么一定会让人有点不舒服？”

The cover creates tension.
The title explains value.
