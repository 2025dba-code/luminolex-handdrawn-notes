# Fish Narration Rules

## Purpose

Generate a continuous spoken narrative that works with the final visual sequence.
Do NOT narrate the image descriptions.

## Style

Default voice:
- Chinese male or neutral professional voice
- mature
- calm
- rational
- experienced consultant / coach-trainer tone
- natural spoken Mandarin

Avoid:
- breath sound instructions
- fake excitement
- excessive rhetorical questions
- repetitive “所以 / 其实 / 那么”
- heavy written-language syntax
- unnecessary English mixing

## Length

Hard ceiling:
- under 15,000 Chinese characters

Preferred:
- 5,000–8,000 characters for ~20 visuals
- longer when source complexity requires it, but preserve pacing

## Structure

The narration should:
1. open with a real tension / misconception
2. explain the key idea
3. use examples
4. identify subtle mistakes
5. provide practical distinctions
6. build toward advanced understanding
7. end with a memorable synthesis

## Visual alignment

Each major visual node should have a natural semantic moment in the narration.
Do not make all transitions identical.

The final Fish draft becomes the ONLY source for semantic anchors.
If the Fish text is later edited, anchors must be regenerated or revalidated.
