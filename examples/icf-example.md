# Example: ICF Competency Chapter

## Source idea

A professional coaching competency chapter explains:
- definition
- PCC indicators
- why the competency matters
- common assessment failures
- examples
- learning points

## Decomposition

Do not force the chapter into 20 images.

Possible visual plan:
1. Competency as a foundation
2. Definition
3. Relationship-level agreement
4. Session-level agreement
5. Session goal
6. Success measure
7. Meaning / importance
8. What needs to be explored
9. Keep the goal central
10. Re-contract when intention changes
11. Common mistake: vague goal
12. Common mistake: coach assumes understanding
13. Common mistake: goal too large for one session
14. Common mistake: goal drift
15. Case visual
16. PCC-level depth
17. MCC-level partnership
18. Learning-point summary
...increase as needed.

## Example prompt

### 图｜目标必须一直在桌面中央

A professional coach and client, both Chinese workplace adults aged 25–40,
sit in a calm coaching conversation.

At the center of the table is one small champagne-gold goal card representing
the agreed session outcome.

Several new topics, insights, and attractive side paths appear around the table
as deep-navy visual elements.

Both coach and client periodically look back toward the central goal card,
using it as a shared reference point.

One new topic appears as a side road, but the coach does not automatically follow it;
instead, the client is shown deciding whether to stay on the current path or recalibrate.

Core Chinese phrase:
“目标始终在中心”

Meaning:
The coach keeps the agreed outcome central unless the client chooses to change direction.

Square 1:1, premium hand-drawn editorial business illustration, warm off-white paper,
deep navy + champagne gold, refined linework, light watercolor / marker texture,
mature Chinese workplace professionals aged 25–40, generous negative space,
no anime, no logo, no large text block.
